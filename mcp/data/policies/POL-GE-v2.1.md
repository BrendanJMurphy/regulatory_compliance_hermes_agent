# Gifts and Entertainment Policy

## Purpose
This policy sets limits on gifts and business entertainment given to or received from persons connected with the firm's business, to prevent improper influence.

## Gift Limits
No employee may give or receive gifts with an aggregate value exceeding USD 100 per recipient per calendar year from any single source. Gifts of cash or cash equivalents are prohibited regardless of value. Promotional items of nominal value bearing a company logo are excluded from the limit.

## Business Entertainment
Business entertainment is permitted where the host is present, the event is not so frequent or lavish as to raise a question of propriety, and the entertainment is pre-cleared in the Compliance system when the estimated value exceeds USD 250 per attendee.

## Reporting
All gifts and entertainment above USD 50 must be logged in the Gifts and Entertainment register within five business days.
