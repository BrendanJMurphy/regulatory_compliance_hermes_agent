# Gifts and Entertainment Policy

## Purpose
This policy sets limits on gifts and business entertainment.

## Gift Limits
No employee may give or receive gifts with an aggregate value exceeding USD 100 per recipient per calendar year from any single source.

## Business Entertainment
Business entertainment is permitted where the host is present and the event is not so frequent or lavish as to raise a question of propriety.
