# Best Execution Policy

## Scope
Applies to all orders in equities, fixed income, and listed derivatives executed on behalf of client accounts.

## Execution Factors
The trading desk must consider price, costs, speed, likelihood of execution and settlement, size, nature, and any other relevant consideration. For retail-classified clients, total consideration (price plus costs) is the primary factor.

## Best Execution Reviews
The Best Execution Committee reviews execution quality quarterly using transaction cost analysis reports. Reviews are minuted and exceptions are tracked to closure. Broker lists are reassessed at least annually.

## Record Keeping
Execution quality reports and committee minutes are retained for seven years.
