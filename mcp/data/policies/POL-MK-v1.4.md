# Marketing and Advertising Review Policy

## Scope
Covers any communication that offers advisory services or that promotes the firm to more than one person, including performance advertising, testimonials, and endorsements.

## Pre-Approval
All marketing materials must be approved by a designated compliance reviewer before first use. Performance figures must show net returns alongside any gross returns over one, five, and ten year periods.

## Testimonials and Endorsements
Testimonials and endorsements require clear disclosure of whether the promoter is a client, whether compensation was paid, and any material conflicts of interest.

## Books and Records
A copy of every approved advertisement and its approval record is retained for at least five years from last use.
